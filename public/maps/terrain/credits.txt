Alappuzha elevation data

Mapzen Terrain Tiles accessed 19 September 2026 from the AWS Open Data Registry:
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/

SRTM and GMTED2010 terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey.
Global ETOPO1 terrain data: U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration;
DOC/NOAA/NESDIS/NCEI, National Centers for Environmental Information.

Source attribution and terms:
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md

Processing: Terrarium zoom 12 pixels decoded to meters, bilinearly resampled
on a local 50-meter grid, quantized to decimeters in little-endian int16.
No vertical exaggeration. Grid spacing is not a claim of survey accuracy.
Source elevations may include vegetation, buildings, and bathymetry.
