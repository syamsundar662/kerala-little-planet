# Kerala village color direction

Reference sources:
- Kerala Tourism village gallery: https://www.keralatourism.org/highresolutionimages/village-life/
- Traditional homestead photograph: https://www.manoramaonline.com/homestyle/nest/2025/01/03/nostalgic-memories-about-old-ancestral-house-and-family.html
- Kerala homestead and coconut grove: https://www.outlooktraveller.com/News/keralas-responsible-tourism-earns-coveted-spot-on-the-unwto-list

Art direction: varied olive and mid-green grasses, deeper tree canopies, aged warm lime plaster, clay tile roofs, dark timber, earthy road verges and subdued reflective water. These are qualitative reference choices, not calibrated color samples; photographs vary with lighting and processing.

Implementation: shared sRGB palette, native photographic leaf texture colors, no daytime leaf emission, neutralized daylight fill, restrained exposure. Terrain and FPV share the ground tint. The approved teal presentation background is retained. No additional postprocessing render pass is introduced.
